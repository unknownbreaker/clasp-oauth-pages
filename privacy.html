Privacy policy

"clasp project" is a personal OAuth client used only by its owner, with their own Google account, to manage their own Google Apps Script projects through clasp.

  • It accesses only the owner's own Google account data, and only to deploy, run and read logs for the owner's scripts and to manage the owner's Gmail settings.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any data with anyone. Credentials stay on the owner's computer.
  • No other users are permitted or supported.
